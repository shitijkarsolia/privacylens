CHALLENGE FRAMES -- PICK ONE
Frame your problem in one of these four areas. Each has a required guardrail your build must satisfy.
Frame Required Guardrail
Education The Agency Guardrail: Your app must solve a problem where the AI is the
scaffolding, not the solution. It must empower a learner to do something they
could not do before, without doing it for them.

Economics The Transparency Guardrail: Your app must expose a hidden economic factor.
Whether it is taxes, environmental debt, or social capital, the spec must focus on
turning invisible data into actionable financial insight.

Ethics The Inclusion Guardrail: Your app must actively mitigate a specific human bias
or privacy risk. Your Kiro spec must include an Ethics Logic Gate, a piece of
code that stops the process if a privacy rule is violated.

Environment The Accountability Guardrail: Your app must bridge the gap between a macro-
problem (climate change) and a micro-action (buying a coffee). The spec must

rely on verifiable data points, not just vibes.
